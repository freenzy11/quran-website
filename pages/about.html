About

About and credits

Al-Furqon is a free reading, memorizing and khatm companion. No account or ads today.

Qur’an text

Arabic text (Uthmani, mushaf pages 1–604 for Read mode), word-by-word transliteration, Tajweed annotations and your chosen translation are provided by the Quran Foundation Content APIs. The translation author and selected reciter are credited with the content. Automatic browser translation is switched off so the vetted translations stay as their authors intended.

Qur’anic glyphs use the QCF V4 (Tajwid on) or QCF V2 (Tajwid off) page font supplied by Quran Foundation; Amiri is only a fallback while a page font loads. Tajwid colours are a lightweight visual guide derived from the written text, not a certified tajwid mushaf.

Recitation

Streamed recitations belong to the reciter you select — Mishary Alafasy, Mahmoud Al-Husary or Mohamed Al-Minshawi. The older Ali Al-Hudhaify preference is still accepted, but Quran Foundation does not publish his ayah-by-ayah audio, so that choice falls back to Mishary Alafasy. Thank you to the reciters for their audio; verse and word clips are streamed on demand and never copied to this site.

Your data

Bookmarks, memorized ayahs, khatm and statistics are saved only in this browser. Clearing the browser’s site data removes them.

Memorization records are stored privately in this browser only.
