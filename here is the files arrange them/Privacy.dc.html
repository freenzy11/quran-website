Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Al-Furqon collects, why, and how it's used. We try to collect as little as we reasonably can.

1. Information we collect

DataWhy we collect it
Email address (and Google account ID, if you sign in with Google)To create and secure your account, and to send sign-in codes
Bookmarks, reading position, memorization progress, streaksSo your place and progress are saved across sessions and devices
Basic usage data (e.g. app crashes, general usage patterns)To fix bugs and keep the Service working reliably

We don't collect payment information, and we don't run advertising or analytics trackers that profile you across other sites.

2. Third-party services we use

We use the Quran Foundation API to provide recitation audio and translations for the Qur'an. When you open a surah or play a recitation, a request (such as which surah/ayah and reciter or translation you selected) is sent to that API to retrieve the content. This request does not include your name or email — it's about the content you're requesting, not you personally.

We use Google Sign-In if you choose that option; Google's own privacy policy governs what Google does with your Google account data during that process.

3. How we store your data

Your account data (email, bookmarks, progress, streaks) is stored securely on our servers and is only used to operate the Service for you. We don't sell, rent, or share this data with advertisers or data brokers.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your personal data (email, bookmarks, progress) within a reasonable time, except where we're required to keep limited records for legal or security reasons.

5. Your choices

  • You can review and update your account email at any time.
  • You can request a copy of your stored data or ask us to delete your account entirely by emailing us.
  • You can stop using the Service at any time; this doesn't delete your data automatically — contact us if you want that done.

6. Children's privacy

Al-Furqon is intended for general audiences. We don't knowingly collect personal information from young children beyond what's needed to operate an account, and we don't use any data for advertising.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date above.

8. Contact

Questions, data requests, or concerns about your privacy? Email us at alfurqon.contact@gmail.com.
